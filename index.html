Everything in one place

The whole operation, organized.

Nine connected core modules replace the scattered spreadsheets, binders, and reminder notes that PPEC compliance usually lives in — then add only the apps your center needs.

Dashboard

Active counts, upcoming recertifications, and credential alerts the moment you sign in.

Patients & demographics

Medicaid ID, DOB, guardian, admission, and status for every child in your care.

Clinical documents

Upload and store Plans of Care, physician orders, and clinical files per patient.

PA & POC tracking

Authorization dates with the Plan-of-Care start auto-calculated 40 days prior, in a live work queue.

Employee files

Personnel records with automatic flags for license, BLS, medical-exam, and fingerprint expirations.

Compliance audits

The full PPEC center self-audit — cross-referenced to your state's regulation set — with deficiencies and corrective-action plans, ready to print.

State agency review

Facility licenses, inspection reports, and policy manuals organized for your state surveyor (AHCA in Florida) — with custom sections you control and PDFs that open in place.

User management

Administrator and office-staff logins with role-based access — no IT department required.

Import & exports

Load your existing rosters in one step; export tracking and personnel data to Excel anytime.

Add-on apps

Extend the core with the apps your center actually uses.

Each app runs on your own instance, shares your patient and staff records, and is customized to your forms, your workflow, and your state's requirements.

Intake & e-Signatures

Digital admission packet

Every consent and admission form, signed on screen by the parent or guardian.

  • All center consents, customized to your forms
  • Parent handbook copy with acknowledgment
  • Guardian ID upload and pre-admission documents (e.g., power of attorney)
  • Annual re-signature reminders, separate from the Plan-of-Care cycle
Clinical Notes

Nursing & clinical documentation

Daily nursing notes and clinical records tied to each patient's chart, with unlimited nursing accounts.

  • Structured note templates per your state's documentation rules
  • Filed to the patient record and mirrored to Drive
  • Priced by census, not by seat
Attendance

Weekly attendance

A simple weekly attendance app for the front desk that feeds billing and census reporting.

  • Daily check-in / check-out by patient
  • Weekly archive with an admin authorization step
  • Exports to Excel
Medicaid Payment Builder

Billing, ready to submit

Turn authorized attendance weeks into clean Medicaid claims with full billing history.

  • Admin authorizes the week; your biller is notified it's ready
  • State-specific procedure codes and modifiers
  • Billing history, sample data loading, and patient uploads inside the app
Incoming

Scanner & document intake

Scan from your office copier or email a PDF and it appears in ppec.soft, ready to be assigned.

  • View, edit, and assign scans to a patient or employee
  • Authorized senders only — unknown senders are ignored
  • Automatic filing to the matching Drive folder

Transportation

  • Attendance sync on arrival

Integrations

Connected to the tools you already have.

No new silos. ppec.soft works with the Google Workspace, copier, spreadsheets, and payroll or billing systems your center already runs on.

Google Drive mirroring

Every patient, employee, and facility record links to its own Drive folder. Anything saved in ppec.soft is copied there automatically — under a signed Google Workspace HIPAA Business Associate Amendment — so your documents are never locked inside one system.

Scan-to-email & copier intake

Point your HP, Canon, or Xerox copier at a dedicated intake address. Scans land in your Incoming queue within minutes, ready to be assigned to a chart or a personnel file.

Excel import & export

Load existing patient, authorization, and staff rosters in one step, including exports from your state's background-screening clearinghouse. Export tracking and personnel data back to Excel any time.

Apps menu for your other systems

One-click links to your payroll, billing, and HR systems from inside ppec.soft — managed by our team, so staff always land in the right place.

Two-factor authentication

Authenticator-app (TOTP) second factor for every login, with hardware-key support available for administrators.

Support by email and text

A named customer-support specialist for your center, online demo scheduling, and training sessions booked in 45-minute slots.
